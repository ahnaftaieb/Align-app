Help & Instructions

How to operate your productivity workspace

TODAY: Daily Hub

Your daily hub. Mark up to 10 daily habits and any situational to-do tasks for today.

SALAT: Prayer Logs

A dedicated checklist to maintain consistency and track offering states across your five daily prayers (Jama'at, On Time, Qada).

ACADEMY: Course Tracks

For long-term goals. Add a custom curriculum track (e.g. '30 Classes'), tap to expand, and use the card's progress grid checkbox buttons to reach 100%.

DASHBOARD: Analytics Matrix

This spreadsheet-style Matrix grid tracks your daily habits, letting you clearly visualize consistency over weeks and months.

FOCUS ZONE: Pomodoro Clock

Set your custom work and break intervals. Link the timer to a specific active Habit or Academy Course dropdown item to record hyper-consistent deep focus.

MANAGE: Control Center

Create and delete habits, register course syllabi, select themes, or empty tracks cleanly to curate your perfect progress suite.
